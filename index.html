FAQ

Good to know

What is in the breakfast bowl?

Oats, chia, pumpkin and sunflower seeds, black raisins and almonds, soaked with protein powder and topped with fresh fruit such as banana, apple and pomegranate.

How do I store it and how long does it stay fresh?

Keep it refrigerated and eat it on the day of delivery. The label on your bowl shows when to consume it by.

Can I choose milk or skip the protein powder?

Yes. Choose water or milk soak, with or without protein powder, and add honey. The price and nutrition update as you choose.

How and when do I order?

Add your bowl to the bag and send the order on WhatsApp by for delivery the next morning. Our team confirms the order and payment details.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Message us on WhatsApp before the cut-off and we will update your order.

I have an allergy. Is it safe?

Our bowls contain nuts and seeds and may contain milk. Please tell us about any allergy before you order.
